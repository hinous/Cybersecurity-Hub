# Scope, Goals, and Risk Assessment Report

## 🎯 Scope and Goals
* **Scope:** The entire security program at Botium Toys, including all assets, internal processes, and procedures.
* **Goals:** Assess existing assets and complete a controls and compliance checklist to determine necessary implementations to improve the company's security posture.

## ⚠️ Risk Assessment summary
* **Risk Description:** Inadequate management of assets and lack of proper security controls to meet U.S. and international regulations.
* **Risk Score:** 8/10 (High).
* **Potential Impact:** Significant financial impact due to potential fines from governing bodies (PCI DSS, GDPR) and loss of customer trust due to data exposure.

## 🖥️ Assets Managed by IT
* **On-premises equipment:** End-user devices (desktops, laptops, smartphones), remote workstations, and surveillance cameras.
* **Systems and Services:** Accounting, telecommunication, database, security, ecommerce, and inventory management.
* **Legacy Systems:** End-of-life systems that require manual monitoring.
* **Data:** Data retention and storage systems (PII, SPII, and Cardholder Data).

## 🚩 Key Vulnerabilities Identified
* **Unauthorized Access:** All employees currently have access to sensitive customer PII/SPII and cardholder information.
* **Data in Plain Text:** Credit card information is not encrypted during processing or storage.
* **Lack of Redundancy:** No disaster recovery plans or critical data backups are currently in place.
* **Weak Authentication:** Password policies do not meet security standards and there is no centralized management.
